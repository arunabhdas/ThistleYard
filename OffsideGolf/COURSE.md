# Whispering Coast — nine-hole course

One continuous fictional coastal estate, loaded one hole at a time. Inland meadows lead through gardens and a river valley to a lighthouse headland. Reuse visible distant landmarks from geographically plausible directions. All lengths are tee-to-green route lengths, not necessarily straight-line distance. Total: par 35, 2,848 yd. Wind directions below describe where air travels toward, matching the simulation convention.

The current implementation bundles all nine geometric files under `_OffsideGolf-frontend-iOS/App/Resources/Courses/Holes/`, generated by `Tools/author_course.py`. The per-hole briefs and diagrams below retain design intent; the committed JSON is authoritative for current collision geometry and placements. Full-course core acceptance covers the actual resources, while final visual/UI and physical-device review remain separate gates.

The shipped default wind is the midpoint of each range, with Hole 2 at 2 mph. Ranges are authoring/tuning limits, not per-frame gusts. The first course version uses fixed values for fair personal-best comparisons. First-play tutorial prompts do not change physics.

Diagram key: `T` tee, `G` green/pin, `.` safe fairway, `r` rough, `B` bunker, `~` water, `Y` tree, `+` bridge, `L` landmark. Diagrams are design briefs, not collision geometry; JSON metre bounds are authoritative.

## 1 — Meadow Start

```text
       L windmill          distant coast ~~~
        r G r                           ~~~
       r ... r                          ~~~
       r ... r      flowers             ~~~
       r ... r
         T
```

- Par 3, 158 yd; northbound straight approach; 36–42 yd fairway width and approximately 28×22 yd green.
- Terrain: tee, fairway, light rough, flat green. No required hazards or tree collisions near the intended route. Ocean is background outside playable bounds.
- Landmark: cream windmill across a wildflower meadow; lighthouse distant enough to establish the destination.
- Tutorial: target drag, swing pad, release, then fine putting. Prompt shows one action at a time. Automatic 5 Iron recommendation reaches the green at moderate power; 7 Iron leaves a short chip.
- Wind 0 mph, elevation within 1 m, green slope ≤0.3%. Wide recovery areas on both sides. No bunker on the beginner landing zone.
- Acceptance: a novice following prompts can reach the green in ≤3 shots; a bad first shot still has a straightforward recovery; prompt replay does not reset scores.

## 2 — Orchard Bend

```text
      G ..       L orchard shed
       r ... Y Y
          ... Y
          ...
           T
```

- Par 4, 325 yd; gentle left dogleg, corner about 190 yd from tee; fairway 30–36 yd.
- Terrain: fairway/rough, small deep-rough pocket inside corner. Orchard trees introduce visible obstacles; no water carry or green bunker.
- Landmark: weathered green shed and terraced fruit trees; coast glimpsed through branches.
- Tutorial: open club selector, compare approximate ranges and accept/override suggestion. Do not force a poor club choice merely to satisfy the tutorial.
- Wind 1–2 mph east; green slope 0.5% toward front. A conservative iron layup avoids the inside canopy.
- Acceptance: both long drive toward corner and two controlled iron shots offer clear routes; manual override remains selected throughout aiming.

## 3 — Ocean Point

```text
          G B       ~~~~~
        ... r       ~~~~~
      ...  r cliff  ~~~~~
      ...           ~~~~~
       T     L lookout
```

- Par 4, 365 yd; broad right bend parallel to cliffs; 32–38 yd fairway, wide inland recovery.
- Terrain: fairway, rough, one small rear-right bunker; sea is playable water beyond cliff edge. No mandatory ocean carry.
- Landmark: timber lookout and distant sailboats; avoid a second lighthouse.
- Tutorial: wind arrow, magnitude and the difference between target and wind-adjusted landing area. Prompt suggests aiming inland, without moving the aim automatically.
- Wind 3–5 mph east toward sea; gentle flat approach; green slope 0.6% inland.
- Drops: inland cliff-path markers at safe heights, never closer to pin than the crossing. Pre-shot fallback if none qualifies.
- Acceptance: preview visibly shifts downwind; choosing a safe inland target remains viable across the entire wind range.

## 4 — Walled Garden

```text
       L old stone arch
        B G B
        r . r
          .
         ...
          T
```

- Par 3, 145 yd; direct approach to a green with left/right bunkers, open front apron and 26 yd central landing lane.
- Terrain: tee, fairway, bunker, rough and green. Low garden walls are scenery outside shot corridors; they do not create invisible collisions.
- Landmark: ivy-covered arch, lavender beds and a small reflecting pool outside play.
- Wind 1–3 mph south (headwind); green slope 0.8% toward front. Sand Wedge recommendation on bunker lie.
- Challenge: choose enough carry without overshooting into back rough; open apron supports a safe short approach.
- Acceptance: bunker slows a rolling ball clearly and emits sand feedback; the golfer can always escape a valid bunker lie without repeated automatic penalties.

## 5 — Clover Valley

```text
              G
          ... B
        ...
       ... rrr L farmhouse
         ...
          ...
           T
```

- Par 5, 475 yd; broad S fairway with landing shelves around 205 and 360 yd. Width 34–44 yd.
- Terrain: fairway, moderate/deep rough pockets, one late inside-corner bunker; no mandatory water. Valley descends 3 m then climbs 2 m.
- Landmark: white farmhouse, grazing silhouettes beyond bounds and clover banks.
- Wind 2–4 mph north; green slope 1%. Challenge is planning the second shot, not adding an unusually long animation.
- Safe route: driver to first shelf, iron to second, wedge/putt finish; an aggressive second shot can challenge the bunker.
- Acceptance: both shelves are reachable with available clubs; deep rough changes expected range and recommended club without random errors.

## 6 — Heron Crossing

```text
              G
             ...
      ~~~~~~~   ~~~~~~~
      ~~~~~~ + ~~~~~~~~
        .......      L heron hide
          ...
           T
```

- Par 4, 340 yd; river crosses the approach near 240 yd; 18–24 yd water span, 36 yd safe landing area beyond it.
- Terrain: fairway, rough, water, green, shallow decorative reed edges. Water/shoreline collision boundary is unambiguous.
- Landmark: wooden bridge and birdwatching hide. Bridge route is a broad playable fairway neck with explicit surface geometry; decorative rails do not trap balls.
- Wind 3–5 mph southwest; mostly level green. Two routes: direct carry from a safe layup or longer ground-friendly route through the neck.
- Drops: authored bank positions, validated outside water/tree volumes. Splash and penalty explanation appear once.
- Acceptance: a cautious player can finish without an unavoidable long carry; swept collision catches even a low fast shot crossing water between ticks.

## 7 — Bellflower Rise

```text
         L bell pavilion
           G / uphill green
          ...
         ... B
          ...
           T
```

- Par 3, 165 yd; pin 6 m above tee; fairway 30 yd wide and green 25×20 yd.
- Terrain: smoothly rising fairway, rough, one right-front bunker, green slope up to 1.5%. No cliff edge directly behind the cup.
- Landmark: small original timber bell pavilion with blue wildflowers, not a copied architectural composition.
- Wind 2–4 mph west; preview includes the actual elevation. One short hint explains uphill shots if the player opens the info chip.
- Challenge: select sufficient carry, then read a gentle side slope while putting.
- Acceptance: trajectory intersects elevated ground correctly, shadows remain attached to terrain, and a stationary ball does not jitter on a supported slope.

## 8 — Pine Passage

```text
           Y G Y
         Y ... Y
      Y ... Y Y
      Y ... Y     L ranger cabin
        ... Y
       Y ... Y
          T
```

- Par 5, 485 yd; double dogleg, 24–30 yd fairway; strategic clearings at 190 and 340 yd.
- Terrain: fairway, rough, deep rough, trees and one optional approach bunker. Trunks never fully block the safe centreline.
- Landmark: moss-roofed ranger cabin and a break in the pines revealing the coast.
- Wind 3–6 mph southeast, not artificially zeroed under canopy. Tree heights 8–14 m; keep route clear for all legal recovery positions.
- Challenge: placement beats maximum power. High shots may clear some trees, while visible canopy collisions slow low shots predictably.
- Acceptance: camera fades foreground foliage over the ball; a legal lie always has an escape direction; no repeated canopy damping while inside the same volume.

## 9 — Lighthouse Home

```text
        L lighthouse        ~~~~
         B G B              ~~~~
          ... r             ~~~~
        ... r               ~~~~
       ...    inlet ~~~~~~~~~~~~
        ...                 ~~~~
         T
```

- Par 4, 390 yd; left-to-right headland curve; 28–34 yd fairway, 24×20 yd green, open front access.
- Terrain: fairway, rough, two small greenside bunkers, water inlet off the aggressive route, green slope ≤2%.
- Landmark: signature cream lighthouse with a terracotta roof; warmer late-afternoon light, still readable hazard colors.
- Wind 4–6 mph east; safe inland route takes an extra setup shot, aggressive route challenges inlet/crosswind.
- Challenge: combine range, wind, hazard planning and putting without a new mechanic. Course completion uses a restrained celebration and full scorecard.
- Acceptance: both routes are viable at wind extremes; final result persists before navigation; replaying a hole cannot manufacture a better nine-hole total.

## Implemented authoring format

`_OffsideGolf-frontend-iOS/App/Resources/Courses/whispering-coast.json` is the manifest. `Tools/author_course.py` reads its yardages, scales each authored route to the intended centreline length, constructs fairway boundaries/regions, and exports `Holes/whispering-coast-01.json` through `-09.json`. Run `python3 OffsideGolf/Tools/author_course.py` from the repository root (the script also resolves paths independently of the current directory). Review generated JSON before accepting content changes; manual output edits must also be carried back into the generator or regeneration will replace them.

All runtime coordinates, bounds, radii and heights are **metres**, with x east and y north. `bounds` is the actual key; neither `boundsMeters` nor normalized 0–1 points are used. The base green is an ellipse in `green`; optional `regions` are polygons with material/priority, not arbitrary shape unions. Camera policy is presentation code and is not a hole JSON field. This valid simplified example shows the live schema; the committed Meadow Start file has more detailed fairway/tee geometry and decoration placement:

```json
{
  "schemaVersion": 1,
  "physicsVersion": 2,
  "contentVersion": 1,
  "id": "whispering-coast-01",
  "par": 3,
  "bounds": {"width": 150, "height": 210},
  "tee": {"x": 75, "y": 30},
  "pin": {"x": 75, "y": 174.4752},
  "safeTarget": {"x": 75, "y": 174.4752},
  "route": [{"x": 75, "y": 30}, {"x": 75, "y": 174.4752}],
  "green": {"center": {"x": 75, "y": 174.4752}, "radiusX": 13, "radiusY": 11},
  "fairway": [{"x": 55, "y": 25}, {"x": 95, "y": 25}, {"x": 95, "y": 180}, {"x": 55, "y": 180}],
  "wind": {"speedMPH": 0, "towardDegrees": 0},
  "elevation": {"origin": {"x": 75, "y": 30}, "baseHeight": 0, "gradient": {"x": 0, "y": 0}},
  "elevationPatches": [],
  "greenSlope": {"x": 0, "y": 0},
  "regions": [],
  "trees": [],
  "drops": [],
  "decorations": [{"asset": "windmill", "position": {"x": 25, "y": 166.4752}, "scale": 1}]
}
```

`HoleDefinition.decode` validates schema, supported physics versions (1/2), finite values, polygon bounds/intersections, unique IDs, route endpoints, tee/pin/drop safety, tree clearance and bounded collection sizes. Optional legacy fields default to calm/flat/empty; missing `physicsVersion` means the original v1 model. Every authored runtime hole explicitly selects v2. Material precedence wins before numeric region priority, with stable IDs breaking remaining ties.

`elevation` is a base plane. Each `elevationPatches` entry supplies `center`, positive `radius` and signed `height`, producing a smooth Gaussian rise/depression. Clover Valley includes a −3 m depression with 60 m radius and a +2 m rise with 45 m radius; these affect live physics and prediction. The green blends into its surroundings and uses its authored `greenSlope` in the interior. These are smooth analytic patches, not a heightmap texture.

Trees specify `id`, `position`, `trunkRadius`, `trunkHeight`, `canopyRadius`, `canopyBottom`, `canopyTop`. Drops specify `id`, `position` and optional `hazardID`; nil applies generically. Some water regions intentionally use the pre-shot fallback when no safe matching/generic drop qualifies. A configured drop cannot improve distance to the pin relative to the crossing and must avoid water, boundaries and tree trunks.

Decoration IDs resolve through `ArtLibrary`: `tree`, `pine`, `windmill`, `shed`, `lookout`, `arch`, `farmhouse`, `hide`, `pavilion`, `cabin`, `lighthouse`, `bridge`. Each hole contains its named landmark. Placements do not themselves create collision bodies: `trees` and `regions` carry gameplay geometry separately.

Default winds are [0, 2, 4, 2, 3, 4, 3, 4.5, 5] mph. Directions use 0° north, 90° east; values are fixed for the hole. The table's ranges are authoring/acceptance limits, not runtime gust fields. Centreline lengths of all nine committed routes match manifest yardage within 0.01 yd in core resource acceptance. Core playability covers nine defaults, seventeen distinct wind endpoints, four aggressive routes and one intentional water-recovery route. These checks establish legal deterministic play, not final difficulty, art approval or a completed full-course UI run.
