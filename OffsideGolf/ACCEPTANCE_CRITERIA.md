## Cross-cutting release regression

Retain the complete matrix below. An exercised simulator row may have partial evidence above, but unchecked release gates must not be marked complete until their actual device/build coverage is recorded.

- [ ] Fresh install, completed/replayed tutorial and returning profile; replay leaves chosen accessibility/input preferences intact.
- [ ] Small/baseline/large iPhone, baseline iPad and resizable iPad window; verify minimum supported iOS/iPadOS separately from current simulator runtime.
- [ ] Portrait/landscape at menu, aiming, charging, flight, rolling, result and pause; required actions remain reachable through safe-area and window changes.
- [ ] Largest accessibility text, manual VoiceOver traversal, reduced motion, contrast/non-color hazards, left-handed layout and untimed controls.
- [ ] No network, audio interruption/silent switch/mixing, background/foreground, impact and settled termination, low-memory notification and long session.
- [ ] First/repeated water, bunker recovery, canopy/trunk contact, OOB, high-speed cup miss and valid capture; visible feedback and safe recovery without duplicate scoring.
- [ ] Correct under/over-par wording, incomplete/final scorecards, practice isolation, assisted exclusion and course/content/physics-separated personal bests across upgrades.
- [ ] Destructive new-round/restart confirmation, save/load Retry, explicit backup/historical recovery and preserved unknown-schema data.
- [ ] Human art review: all intended directions/cosmetic combinations, minimum scale, terrain seams/UVs, golfer/ball occlusion, readable guidance, comfortable camera/motion and complete provenance.
- [ ] Baseline Release performance: sustained 60 FPS target, p95 ≤16.7 ms over a representative ten-minute route, no recurrent >33 ms spikes outside loading, cold interactive menu ≤2 s, cached transition ≤1 s, peak working set ≤250 MB and texture budget ≤96 MB. Record measurements; dimension-based estimates are not proof.
- [ ] At least five first-time testers can make their first shot within 30 seconds without a manual; observe actual learning rather than use optimized acceptance inputs as a proxy.
- [ ] Final Release resource/privacy/signing inspection, absent DEBUG UI/APIs/reference images, signed install/upgrade/save tests, store metadata and actual TestFlight processing/launch.

`ART_DIRECTION.md` documents eight authored golfer facings and approximately 66 MiB of source RGBA image planes (about 72 MiB if independent terrain crops are additionally materialized). These values do not measure total CPU/GPU texture residency and do not close the PRD budget gate.
